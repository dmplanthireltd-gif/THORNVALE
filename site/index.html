Life in Thornvale

More than a hunt

Gather and forge

Mine copper to starsteel, smelt bars and hammer out your own armour. Fish the rivers, fell the forests.

Open PvP

Fight anyone outside town. Skulls mark the killers, protection zones keep the peace, secure mode keeps you honest.

Warbands and friends

Form a party, share the spoils and the experience, and keep your friends one click away.

A player market

Post offers on the market boards and trade face to face. Every coin in the realm is earned by a player.

79 spells and runes

Waves of fire, beams of energy, walls of magic and runes that turn a fight. Learn them from the mages in town.

Nothing to install

Open the page and play. Your character is saved on the server, so you can pick it up from any computer.
