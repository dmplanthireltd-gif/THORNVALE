The world

Towers above. Dungeons below. Danger everywhere in between.

Every creature, wall and sword is built in real 3D and drawn in a crisp pixel-art style. Climb ladders, use a rope to escape caves, dig through loose stones with a shovel and sail between ports.

Towns and trade

Shops, banks, a depot you can reach from any locker, and safe trading between players.

Fortresses and hunting grounds

Walls, gates and archer towers to storm, with monsters that kite, heal and shoot back.

The deep dungeons

Floor after floor underground, where the only light is the one you carry.

Vocations

Choose how you fight.

Each vocation grows differently in health, mana and carrying strength, and has its own weapons and spells.

Knight

Heavy armour, swords and axes. Stands at the front and takes the hits.

Paladin

Bows, crossbows and spears. Strikes from range and keeps moving.

Sorcerer

Wands and destructive spells. Burns whole packs of monsters at once.

Druid

Rods and healing magic. Keeps the whole party standing.
