Dead Locks

Somebody left the sound system running down in Bunker 47, and the bass woke the whole garrison. Now they won't stop moving. Hold the barracks, board up the windows, buy your way deeper, and keep the riddim going as long as you last.

Every hit earns points; kills and headshots earn more. Spend them on wall weapons, the Dubplate Crate's random weapon, tonics from the machines, and the steel doors to the Rum Bar, the Sound System Hall, the Record Store and the Dancehall. Clear the stairs in the Sound System Hall to reach the Studio, where the Remix Desk turns any weapon into something far nastier. Every tenth round Deadlock comes for you. Go down in co-op and a teammate has 30 seconds to revive you.
